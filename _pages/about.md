I am a research economist currently completing my MPhil in Economics at the University of Oxford, supervised by Alexandre Kohlhas and Michael McMahon. My research uses large-scale household- and firm-level microdata to study how heterogeneity in expectations and decision-making shapes aggregate dynamics.

Before my masters studies, I worked in the research department of the Reserve Bank of Australia, and previously graduated from the University of Sydney with First Class honours in Economics and Physics.
